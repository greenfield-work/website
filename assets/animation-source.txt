Greenfield workflow animation

Custom small gardener character, work cap, clothing, dense grass, fields,
planting poses, turns, and hands-behind-head resting pose,
lighting and rendering prepared for Greenfield in Blender.

Walk motion is adapted from the Walk action in the official Three.js example:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
The source soldier mesh and textures are not included in the rendered artwork.
The source motion clip uses a Mixamo skeleton. Movement speed is synchronized
with its grounded foot phase. No third-party scripts or 3D models load in the
visitor's browser. The transparent render is packed as color on the left and
a grayscale alpha mask on the right of one H.264 video. A small WebGL
compositor restores transparency; the static WebP fallback also has alpha.

The accompanying Blender source and render scripts are kept in the local
Greenfield motion study, separate from this static website package.
